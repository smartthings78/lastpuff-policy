LastPuff — Privacy Policy

App: LastPuff: Quit Vaping (com.grounded.lastpuff) · Developer: Setminded · Effective: July 9, 2026

The short version

LastPuff collects no personal data. Everything you enter — your vaping habits, quit date, cravings, slips, check-ins, your "why" — is stored only on your device. We have no servers holding your data, no accounts, no ads, no tracking SDKs, and we never sell data. We couldn't sell your data if we wanted to, because we never receive it.

What the app stores (on your device only)

Your quiz answers (device type, usage amount, spend, triggers, quit history, motivation), your quit date and path (taper or cold turkey), puff logs, slips and shields, daily check-ins (mood and urge ratings), panic-button usage, audio-session completion, and settings. This data lives in the app's local storage on your phone and is included in your device backups according to your own phone settings.

What we collect on our servers

Nothing. The core app works entirely offline.

Crisis keyword scanning

If you write an optional note during a check-in, the app scans it on your device for signs of crisis so it can show support resources (such as the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline in the US). The note is never stored and never transmitted anywhere.

Purchases

Subscriptions, if you choose one, are processed by Google Play or Apple. We receive anonymized transaction confirmations from those platforms to unlock premium features; we do not receive your payment details.

Community (when available)

If a community feature ships in a future version, posts will be anonymous by default under a generated handle, moderated, with no private messaging. Any change to data handling will be reflected in this policy before that feature launches.

Children

LastPuff is intended for users 18 and over. Younger users who use the app to quit receive a restricted experience (read-only community, no optional data entry requested) and, like everyone, have no data collected.

How long we keep it

We do not store or retain any of your data. The core app is entirely offline and we operate no server that holds your quiz answers, quit date, puff logs, check-ins or notes. There is no account and no cloud sync, so we have no copy to keep and no retention period to define on our side.

Your data stays in the app's local storage on your phone for as long as you choose to keep it, and is gone the moment you erase it in Settings or uninstall the app. Note that it is included in your own device backups if you have those switched on — that copy is controlled by your phone's backup settings, not by us. Crisis-scan notes are never stored at all, on the device or anywhere else. Purchase confirmations are held by Google Play or Apple under their own retention policies, not ours.

Your controls

In Settings you can edit or delete any entry, export all of your data as JSON, and permanently erase everything with one tap. Because there is no server copy, deletion on your device is total.

Not medical advice

LastPuff supports your quit; it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a healthcare professional about nicotine replacement therapy or other treatment.

Changes & contact
